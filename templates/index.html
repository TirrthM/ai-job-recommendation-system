Why Content-Based vs. Collaborative Filtering?

Collaborative Filtering (CF) relies on user interaction matrices (e.g., job application histories, clicks, ratings). In student or job boards, new jobs and fresh alumni experience the Cold Start Problem.

Content-Based Filtering (CBF) circumvents this by analyzing intrinsic item attributes (skills, experience, job descriptions). Every candidate receives recommendations immediately upon profile creation.

Mathematical Formulation of Naive Bayes

The classifier uses Bayes' Theorem with conditional feature independence:

$$P(C_k \mid x) = \frac{P(C_k) \prod_{i=1}^{n} P(x_i \mid C_k)}{P(x)}$$

Where $C_k$ is the Job Title category and $x_i$ represents the TF-IDF feature weight of a specific tech skill.
